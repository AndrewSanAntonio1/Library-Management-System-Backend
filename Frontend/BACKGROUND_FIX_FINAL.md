Replaced the complex animated Three.js library background with:


**Colors:**
- `from-blue-50` - Very light blue (#eff6ff)
- `via-purple-50` - Very light purple (#faf5ff)  
- `to-pink-50` - Very light pink (#fdf2f8)

**Result:** Beautiful pastel gradient that stays LIGHT and STATIC!
